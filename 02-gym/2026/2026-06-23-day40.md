## Key MMC / Form Insights
- `Leg Press Machine`:
  - Lower foot placement and thinking “push with thighs” improved quad burn.
  - Short rest from social pressure pushed one set to RPE 10 and raised heart rate.
  - Lesson: rest time is part of training; okay to ask to rest 2 more minutes or let the other person go first.

- `Leg Extension Machine`:
  - Biggest breakthrough: knees were previously a bit far from the seat/pad setup.
  - Moving the backrest farther so the knees sat closer / better aligned with the pad made the quad stimulus much better.
  - Set 4 was MVP because setup + MMC improved stimulus without needing higher load.

- `Standing Calf Extension Machine`:
  - Important cue learned: keep knees soft, do not lock them hard.
  - Soft knees improved control and likely reduced joint stress.
  - Calf felt properly tired after 25kg working sets.
