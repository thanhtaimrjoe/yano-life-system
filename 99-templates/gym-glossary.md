# Gym Glossary

This glossary standardizes gym terminology for consistent use by AI (Codex, ChatGPT Project, etc.) and easy reference.

## Core Training Terms

| Term              | Definition                                                                 | Notes / Vietnamese                  | Example in Context                          |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Rep**           | One complete movement of an exercise (e.g., one squat or one press).      | Lần lặp                              | "Do 10 reps per set"                       |
| **Set**           | A group of consecutive reps.                                              | Hiệp                                 | "3 sets of 12 reps"                        |
| **RPE**           | Rate of Perceived Exertion (1-10 scale). How hard the set felt.           | Mức độ nỗ lực chủ quan               | "RPE 7-8" (could do 2-3 more reps)         |
| **RIR**           | Reps In Reserve. How many more reps you could do with good form.          | Số rep còn dư                        | "RIR 2-3"                                  |
| **DOMS**          | Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness. Muscle soreness that appears 24-72h later. | Đau cơ chậm                          | "Expected DOMS in quads tomorrow"          |
| **MMC**           | Mind-Muscle Connection. Consciously feeling and activating the target muscle. | Kết nối tâm-thần cơ                  | "Focus on MMC in the lats during pulldown" |
| **Eccentric**     | The lowering/lowering phase of the movement (lengthening the muscle).     | Giai đoạn hạ / giãn cơ               | "Control the eccentric for 3 seconds"      |
| **Concentric**    | The lifting/shortening phase of the movement.                             | Giai đoạn nâng / co cơ               | "Explode on the concentric"                |
| **Tempo**         | The speed of each phase of the rep (e.g., 3-1-2).                         | Nhịp độ                              | "Tempo 3-0-2" (3s eccentric, 0 pause, 2s concentric) |
| **Form / Technique** | Proper body positioning and movement pattern to target muscles safely.   | Kỹ thuật / form                      | "Prioritize good form over heavy weight"   |
| **Progressive Overload** | Gradually increasing stress on the body over time (weight, reps, sets, etc.). | Tăng tiến dần                        | "Apply progressive overload once form is solid" |

## Session & Planning Terms

| Term                  | Definition                                                                 | Notes                               | Example                                      |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Focus**             | Main emphasis of the session (e.g., Upper A, Lower B, MMC).               | Tiêu điểm buổi tập                   | "Today's focus: Upper A with chest emphasis" |
| **Comeback Phase**    | Period of returning to training after a long break.                       | Giai đoạn quay lại tập               | "Be conservative in comeback phase"        |
| **Volume**            | Total amount of work (sets × reps × weight).                              | Khối lượng công việc                 | "Keep volume moderate this week"           |
| **Intensity**         | How heavy the weights are relative to your max.                           | Cường độ                             | "Moderate intensity, focus on control"     |
| **Recovery**          | Process of the body adapting and repairing after training.                | Phục hồi                             | "Monitor recovery status before next session" |
| **Warm-up**           | Light activity to prepare the body and joints.                            | Khởi động                            | "Do a proper warm-up before main lifts"    |
| **Cooldown**          | Light activity after training to aid recovery.                            | Hạ nhiệt                             | "10 min treadmill cooldown"                |

## Machine & Setup Terms

| Term              | Definition                                                                 | Notes                               | Example                                      |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Setup**         | Adjusting the machine (seat, pads, handles) for proper alignment.         | Cài đặt máy                          | "Adjust the pad height for better MMC"     |
| **ROM**           | Range of Motion. The full movement path of an exercise.                   | Biên độ chuyển động                  | "Use full ROM without locking out"         |
| **Neutral Grip**  | Palms facing each other.                                                  | Nắm trung lập                        | "Use neutral grip on seated row"           |
| **Pronated Grip** | Palms facing down/away.                                                   | Nắm úp                               | "Pronated grip on lat pulldown"            |
| **Supinated Grip**| Palms facing up.                                                          | Nắm ngửa                             | "Supinated grip on curls"                  |

## Mindset & Coaching Terms

| Term                    | Definition                                                                 | Notes                               | Example                                      |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Form-First**          | Prioritizing correct technique over lifting heavier weights.              | Ưu tiên kỹ thuật trước               | "Stay form-first during comeback"          |
| **No Ego Lifting**      | Avoiding lifting weights that compromise form.                            | Không tập ego                        | "No ego lifting — control the movement"    |
| **Autoregulation**      | Adjusting training based on how you feel that day (energy, recovery).     | Tự điều chỉnh                        | "Use autoregulation if feeling fatigued"   |
| **Session Rating**      | Overall quality rating of the workout (e.g., 8/10).                       | Đánh giá buổi tập                    | "Session rating: 9/10 — great MMC today"   |
