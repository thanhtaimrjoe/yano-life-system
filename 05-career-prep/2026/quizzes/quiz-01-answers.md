## Common Mistakes to Watch For

### Terminology Confusion
- **商材 vs 商品:** 商材 is category (top-level), 商品 is product (individual item)
- **資料 vs ツール:** Both refer to documents, semantically equivalent
- **現行品 vs 終息:** Current vs discontinued products

### Translation Errors
- **図面 ≠ document:** 図面 = zumen (technical drawings), not generic "document"
- **部品 ≠ product:** 部品 = parts (components), not products
- **取説 ≠ technical:** 取説 = description/manual, 技術資料 = technical

### Counting Errors
- **Document types:** 12 types total (not 9 or 10)
- **Product categories:** 7 categories with dedicated UI (not 5 or 8)
- **Brands:** 4 mentioned (LIXIL, TOEX, トステム, 新日軽)
