## Movement Quality & Insights
- Key cues applied successfully:
  - `V-handle Pulldown`: shoulder down first, hands as hooks, elbows down toward ribs/hips, no over-deep pulling.
  - `Chest Supported Row`: left side first, shoulder down, elbow toward back pocket, grip lighter, match right side to left quality.
  - `Rear Delt Fly`: shoulders down, neck long, avoid pulling too deep, move arms out sideways more than backward.
  - `Machine Preacher Curl`: keep upper arm on pad, avoid shoulder/trap assistance, slow eccentric.
- What improved today:
  - `V-handle Pulldown` produced its clearest lat burn so far.
  - Left-side row improved within the session after initial shoulder takeover.
  - User clearly identified right-side unilateral advantage: right side rows felt much more natural and smooth.
  - User noticed trap takeover risk on rear delt fly and managed it with shoulder-lowering and ROM control.
- Form lesson / realization:
  - For unilateral back work, the right side currently has much better motor control; left side needs slower, cleaner reps and may need to stop before matching right-side depth.
