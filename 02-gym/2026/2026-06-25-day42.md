## Movement Quality & Insights
- Key cues applied successfully:
  - `Romanian Deadlift`: ribs down, hips back, long spine; avoid over-arching / over-sticking the butt out.
  - `Romanian Deadlift`: push through hips instead of forcing a deep stretch through lumbar extension.
  - `Leg Curl`: knees positioned so they do not press/catch the pad incorrectly.
  - `Leg Curl`: heel leads the movement; curl through hamstrings instead of kicking from the knee.
  - `Hip Abduction`: slow motion, squeeze at max range, controlled eccentric.
- What improved today:
  - User identified the difference between RDL hinge and stiff-leg style stretch.
  - Hamstring activation improved when load was reduced and pelvis/rib position was controlled.
  - `Leg Curl` produced the clearest hamstring MMC so far: about 90% target muscle feel.
  - `Hip Abduction` felt clean and effective with 25kg x 15.
- Form lesson / realization:
  - Over-arching the low back to “show the butt” during RDL shifts load away from hamstrings and into the lower back.
  - For the current phase, RDL should stay as a controlled hinge, not a deep stiff-leg stretch chase.
  - `Leg Curl` may be the safer main hamstring stimulus while RDL pattern is still being learned.
