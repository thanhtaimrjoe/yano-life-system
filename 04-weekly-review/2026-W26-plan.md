# 2026-W26 — Training Goals & Plan

## Weekly Intent
- Target: **5 gym sessions** from Monday to Friday.
- Rest: **Saturday + Sunday** as full rest or very light recovery.
- Main goal: stabilize the MMC unlocks from W25 instead of chasing PRs.
- Monday starts with **Chest Technique** because user wants chest today and had 2 rest days over the weekend.
- Guardrail: keep this as a skill-stabilization week. Good form > extra sets > heavier weight.

## Weekly Goals

### 1. Training Consistency
- Complete **5 sessions** if recovery stays normal.
- If trap/neck/shoulder, hamstring, or calf feels off, convert one day into active recovery instead of forcing volume.

### 2. RPE Control
- Main machine sets: mostly **RPE 6-8**.
- Upper-body accessories: hard cap at **RPE 8**.
- No RPE 9-10 on `Cable Lateral Raise`, `Chest Press Machine`, `Rear Delt Fly Machine`, or `Machine Preacher Curl`.

### 3. Chest Confidence
- Rebuild chest pressing confidence without shoulder compensation.
- Use controlled ROM: stop descent when chest stretch is enough, before shoulder takes over.
- `Chest Press Machine` remains form-learning; do not chase 5kg if it feels unstable.

### 4. Back Symmetry
- Add left-side primer before `Chest Supported Row Machine`:
  - 5kg x 8 reps
  - half-ROM
  - 1s hold at back squeeze
- Goal: left side learns “pull by back”, not biceps/shoulder.

### 5. Lower Body Skill
- `Romanian Deadlift`: use **3 x 10-12** instead of 3 x 15.
- Goal: hinge clean, breathing controlled, no low-back weirdness.

### 6. Recovery & Tracking
- Log sleep/recovery at least **3 days** this week.
- Daily protein anchor: at least **1-2 obvious protein sources/day**.
- Saturday/Sunday: rest, walking optional, no compensation workout.
