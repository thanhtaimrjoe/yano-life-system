# BrSE Answer Bank

> Noi gom cac cau tra loi phong van BrSE. Viet ngan, de noi, uu tien mindset dung hon cau chu hoan hao.

---

## Core Mindset

BrSE khong chi la nguoi dich. BrSE can chuyen y dinh kinh doanh cua khach thanh thong tin dev co the implement, dong thoi quan ly ambiguity, risk, expectation va communication giua hai ben.

AI co the ho tro dich, tom tat, soan Q&A va kiem tra logic, nhung BrSE van phai chiu trach nhiem ve understanding, judgement, escalation va trust.

---

```text
Huynh Thanh Taiと申します。

私はソフトウェア工学を専攻し、ITコミュニケーターとQAとしての経験があります。

これまで、要件を確認したり、チーム内に正確に伝えたりする業務を経験しました。
その中で、日本側とベトナムチームの橋渡し役として働くことに興味を持ち、BrSEを目指すようになりました。

これらの強みを活かして、BrSEとして経験を積みながら、プロジェクトに貢献したいと考えております。

よろしくお願いいたします。
```

## Q1. BrSEの一番大事な役割は何ですか

### Vietnamese Draft

Theo em, vai tro quan trong nhat cua BrSE la lam cau noi giua khach hang Nhat va team development Viet Nam. Nhung cau noi o day khong chi la dich ngon ngu, ma la chuyen dung y dinh, boi canh, muc do uu tien va rui ro cua requirement.

AI co the ho tro BrSE rat tot trong viec dich, tom tat, soan tai lieu hoac check logic. Tuy nhien, BrSE van can chiu trach nhiem cuoi cung ve viec hieu dung khach hang, xac nhan diem mo ho, truyen dat cho team va xay dung trust giua hai ben.

## Q2. Requirement mo ho thi xu ly the nao

### Vietnamese Draft

Neu requirement con mo ho, truoc tien em se trao doi voi team dev de lam ro cu the dang thieu thong tin gi: business rule, UI behavior, exception case, data condition hay priority.

Sau do em se cung PM hoac leader phan loai. Diem nao team co the tu quyet theo convention thi xu ly noi bo; diem nao anh huong scope, estimate, quality hoac nghiep vu thi can confirm voi khach hang.

Khi gui khach, em se chuan bi Q&A ro rang, tranh hoi chung chung. Neu co the, em se dua option A/B kem impact de khach de quyet dinh.

---

## Q3. Khach tra loi cham, team bi block, deadline gan

### Vietnamese Draft

Em se tach phan da clear va phan chua clear. Nhung task khong phu thuoc cau tra loi cua khach thi team nen tiep tuc xu ly truoc de tranh lang phi thoi gian cho.

Voi cac diem dang block, em se trao doi voi PM de xem co the dua ra assumption tam thoi hay option xu ly noi bo khong. Neu assumption co rui ro anh huong scope, nghiep vu hoac quality, em se remind khach mot cach ro rang, kem ly do can confirm va impact neu cau tra loi cham.

Neu deadline bi anh huong, em se cung PM bao lai khach bang cac option nhu: giu deadline nhung giam scope, implement tam theo assumption da neu, hoac dieu chinh deadline.

## Q4. Dev noi requirement khong lam duoc, khach van insist

### Vietnamese Draft

Truoc tien em se hoi ro ly do: technical constraint, timeline, existing system limitation, security, performance hay scope ban dau. Em cung se hoi neu bat buoc lam thi impact den cost, quality va schedule la gi.

Sau do em trao doi voi PM/leader de xac nhan rui ro va tim alternative solution co the dap ung business need cua khach. Khi trao doi lai voi khach, em se khong chi noi "khong lam duoc", ma giai thich constraint, impact va de xuat option cu the.

## Q5. Trong meeting khong hieu ro requirement

### Vietnamese Draft

Em se note lai ngay diem chua hieu. Neu diem do anh huong den viec truyen dat cho team hoac estimate, em se hoi lai sau khi khach noi xong, tranh interrupt khong can thiet.

Neu flow meeting chua tien hoi ngay, em se tong hop cuoi buoi bang cach confirm: "Em xin xac nhan lai understanding cua em la...". Voi noi dung lien quan technical feasibility, neu can danh gia sau hon, em se noi voi khach la team can confirm technical impact va feedback lai sau.

---

## Q6. Dich sai / truyen dat thieu requirement

### Vietnamese Draft

Neu em phat hien minh dich sai hoac truyen dat thieu requirement lam dev implement sai, dau tien em se khong che giau ma danh gia impact ngay: sai o phan nao, anh huong den scope, quality, schedule va delivery the nao.

Sau do em bao PM/leader, trao doi voi dev de xac nhan effort sua, kha nang revert hoac phuong an fix nhanh nhat. Neu loi anh huong den khach hoac deadline, em se cung PM communicate lai minh bach: giai thich ngan gon van de, nhan trach nhiem phan communication va dua ra ke hoach sua.

Sau khi xu ly xong, em se review nguyen nhan va cai thien quy trinh: confirm understanding cuoi meeting, ghi Q&A ro hon, update spec/ticket day du hon.
