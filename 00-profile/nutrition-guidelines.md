## Current nutrition direction

Goal phase:
- Reduce fat first
- Keep gym performance and recovery stable
- Build eating pattern that is sustainable for office-life routine

## Protein target

Current body stats used for planning:
- Height: 1.68m
- Weight: 71.2kg
- Waist at navel: 96cm

Suggested daily protein target:
- 115-140g/day
- Increase gradually, not all at once

## Whey status

Current preference:
- If trustworthy whey is unavailable in Vietnam market, prioritize protein from whole foods instead of buying low-trust products.
- Whey is convenience, not mandatory.

## Main food priorities

Each main meal should have a clear protein source.

Preferred protein sources:
- Eggs
- Chicken
- Lean pork
- Beef
- Fish
- Shrimp
- Tofu
- Milk / yogurt if digestion is okay

## Practical rules

- Do not let meals be mostly carbs with no clear protein source.
- After morning gym, prioritize breakfast with stronger protein.
- Before morning gym, eat enough to support the session, especially on pull/lower days.
- Current pre-workout watch item: rice + small protein may feel less effective than sweet potato for stable gym energy; track this as observation before treating it as a fixed rule.
- If no whey, increase protein from meat, eggs, tofu, and dairy where possible.
- Keep fat loss flexible; no extreme restriction needed.
- Gym-first approach is preferred over hard cardio.
- Light post-workout cardio is acceptable for extra calorie burn.

## Easy meal upgrades

Examples:
- Oatmeal + milk -> add 2-3 eggs
- Rice meal -> increase meat/fish/tofu portion
- Snack -> eggs, yogurt, milk, or another simple protein source
- Dinner -> keep one dedicated protein dish, not only rice and vegetables

## Tracking note

- Daily perfection is not required.
- Weekly consistency matters more than single-meal accuracy.
- If calories/macros are unknown, focus on protein presence in each meal.
