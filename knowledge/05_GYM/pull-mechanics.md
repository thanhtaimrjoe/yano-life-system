## Yano Cue™ (General Pull Setup)

Validated on: Assisted Pull-up, Lat Pulldown, Chest Supported Row, One Arm Cable Row, Rear Delt Machine, Biceps Curl Machine.

```text
Inhale
↓
Relax shoulders
↓
Natural scapular depression
↓
Pull with elbows
↓
Reset shoulders
↓
Next rep
```

## Key Lat & Pull Discoveries

### 1. ROM > Weight
Reducing load to zero (e.g., One Arm Cable Row) allows learning proper ROM. Extending the elbow path beyond the hip ("past the back pocket") produces strong lat contraction under the armpit. Proper setup matters more than weight.

### 2. Trap Reset
Upper trap takeover often happens **between reps**, not during reps. Adding a shoulder reset before every repetition eliminates most upper trap involvement.

### 3. MMC is Trainable
Mind-Muscle Connection (especially on the weaker side) can be dramatically improved within a single workout through conscious setup and pre-rep routines (like the Yano Cue), rather than just adding volume.

### 4. Hands are Hooks
Treating hands as hooks and consciously pulling with elbows instead of hands consistently improves lat recruitment and reduces biceps participation.
