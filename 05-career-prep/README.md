## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Do I need to fill in every field in daily log?
**A:** No. Fill in what's relevant. But try to complete the core sections (time blocks, metrics, reflection, tomorrow's plan).

### Q: What if I miss a day?
**A:** Don't break the chain! Even if you only work 2 hours, log it. Consistency > Intensity.

### Q: Can I adjust the schedule?
**A:** Yes! The plan is a guide, not a prison. Adjust based on your energy and progress. But keep the core structure.

### Q: How much AI help is OK?
**A:** Use AI for clarification, not understanding. Try to read specs independently first. The goal is to reduce AI dependency.

### Q: What if I finish early?
**A:** Great! Use extra time for:
- Polish mini-KINKEN
- Add nice-to-have knowledge base sections
- Extra mock interview practice
- Start CV writing early

### Q: What if I'm behind schedule?
**A:** Follow emergency protocol (see above). Focus on priorities. Quality > Quantity.
