# Nutrition Tracking Charter

## Philosophy

**Goal:** Track nutrition for consistency and awareness, NOT perfection.

**Core Principle:** 
- Consistency > Perfect
- Sustainable > Restrictive
- Awareness > Obsession

## What to Track

### Daily Tracking (Essential)

**For each meal, log:**
1. **Protein source** (crab, chicken, fish, egg, tofu, etc.)
2. **Carb source** (rice, noodles, bread, potato, etc.)
3. **Veggies** (yes/no + type)
4. **Oil level** (light/medium/heavy)
5. **Time** (optional but helpful)

**Daily Summary:**
- Protein: ✅ (adequate) / ⚠️ (borderline) / ❌ (low)
- Veggies: ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌
- Oil intake: ✅ (balanced) / ⚠️ (moderate) / ❌ (high)

### Weekly Review (Synthesis)

- Pattern recognition: "Tuần này ăn như thế nào?"
- Oil intake trend: "Bao nhiêu bữa ăn dầu nhiều?"
- Protein consistency: "Có bữa nào thiếu protein không?"
- Challenges: "Ngày nào khó ăn healthy nhất?"

## Sustainable Rules (80/20)

### Rule 1: Protein Every Meal ✅
- Target: 1 protein source per meal
- Examples: crab, chicken, fish, egg, tofu
- Status: Bạn đã làm tốt

### Rule 2: Veggies Most Meals
- Target: 80% bữa ăn có rau
- Minimum: 1 loại rau xanh hoặc rau khác
- Benefit: fullness + fiber + micronutrients

### Rule 3: Oil Intake - 80/20
- 80% bữa ăn: ít dầu (nấu, luộc, hấp)
- 20% bữa ăn: có thể ăn dầu nhiều (chiên, order out)
- Reality: Không cần perfect, chỉ cần balanced

### Rule 4: Carbs - Flexible
- Target: Mỗi bữa có carb
- No low-carb obsession
- Adjust based on hunger + training

## What NOT to Track

❌ Calorie counting (unless you want to)
❌ Macro percentages (unless you want to)
❌ Weight daily (track weekly at most)
❌ "Clean vs dirty" food labels
❌ Guilt about eating

## Tracking Format

**File location:** `03-meals/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

**Template:** Use `99-templates/nutrition-template.md`

**Frequency:** Log after each meal or end of day

**Time investment:** 2-3 minutes per day

## Weekly Nutrition Review

**When:** Every Sunday (part of weekly review)

**What to analyze:**
- Protein consistency: "Có bữa nào thiếu protein?"
- Veggie intake: "Tuần này ăn rau bao nhiêu bữa?"
- Oil pattern: "Bao nhiêu bữa ăn dầu nhiều? Có pattern không?"
- Challenges: "Ngày nào khó nhất? Tại sao?"
- Next week: "Cần adjust gì?"

**Example:**
```
## Weekly Nutrition Summary (W21)

**Protein:** ✅ Consistent, mỗi bữa có
**Veggies:** ⚠️ 5/7 ngày đủ, cần improve
**Oil intake:** ⚠️ Thứ 6 ăn 2 bữa dầu nhiều, nhưng overall balanced
**Challenges:** Weekend order out → dầu nhiều hơn
**Next week:** Meal prep thêm cho weekend

**Pattern:** Ăn tại nhà = ít dầu, order out = dầu nhiều
**Action:** Nấu thêm cho Friday dinner để giảm order out
```

## When You Start Working (Morning Gym Phase)

**Meal timing adjustment:**
- **Pre-gym (5-6am):** Light snack (banana, toast, etc.)
- **Post-gym (7-8am):** Breakfast (protein + carb + veggies)
- **Lunch (12-1pm):** Meal prep from home (protein + carb + veggies)
- **Dinner (6-7pm):** Cook with girlfriend (protein + carb + veggies)
- **Snacks:** As needed

**Meal prep strategy:**
- Sunday: Prep Mon-Fri lunch + some dinners
- Budget: ~500k/week (bạn đã có)
- Time: 2-3 hours Sunday

## Flexibility Rules

**If you break the rules:**
- 1 meal with no veggies? → Not a disaster
- 1 day with high oil? → Not a disaster
- 1 week inconsistent? → Adjust next week

**If you're struggling:**
- Reduce tracking to 3x/week (instead of daily)
- Focus on protein only (skip oil tracking)
- Ask for help (chat with AI)

## Success Metrics

**Not about:**
- Perfect meals
- Zero "unhealthy" food
- Strict meal prep

**About:**
- Protein every meal (80% of time)
- Veggies most meals (80% of time)
- Oil balanced (80% light, 20% heavy)
- Consistency over 4+ weeks

## Integration with Gym Tracking

**Connection:**
- Good nutrition → better recovery → better MMC development
- Protein + training → muscle growth
- Consistency in both → sustainable progress

**Not about:**
- Nutrition perfection = gym perfection
- One bad meal = one bad workout
- Food guilt = training guilt
