Palpation confirmed strong contraction under the armpit.

# Knowledge

## Yano Cue™

```
Inhale

↓

Relax shoulders

↓

Natural scapular depression

↓

Pull with elbows

↓

Reset shoulders

↓

Next rep
```

Validated on:

- Assisted Pull-up
- Lat Pulldown
- Chest Supported Row
- One Arm Cable Row
- Rear Delt Machine
- Biceps Curl Machine

---

## Key Discoveries

### 1. ROM > Weight

Dropping to zero weight for One Arm Cable Row produced better lat activation than forcing heavier loads.

---

### 2. Trap reset

Upper trap wasn't taking over during reps.

It was taking over **between reps**.

Resetting shoulders before every repetition fixed this immediately.

---

### 3. Left MMC can be trained

Left-side MMC improved from approximately **6/10** to **9/10** within a single workout through conscious setup rather than additional volume.

---

### 4. Hands are hooks

Thinking about pulling with elbows instead of hands consistently improved lat recruitment.
