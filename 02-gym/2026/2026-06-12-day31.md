## Key MMC / Form Wins
- Incline Chest Press setup insight:
  - Original seat height targeted upper chest well.
  - Lowering seat shifted tension into shoulder and was not suitable.
- Pec Fly insight:
  - Opening too deep makes shoulder participate.
  - Better ROM: open only until chest stretch is enough, then squeeze back.
  - Max squeeze should not be forced too hard because shoulder can tense to stabilize.
- Lateral Raise insight:
  - Right shoulder should be trained first next time for fairer comparison.
  - 5kg is still heavy for strict controlled side-delt work.
  - If rear delt burns strongly, arm path may be slightly too far back.

## Issues / Lessons
- Today exceeded the plan after Pec Fly and Cable work.
- RPE target was 7-8 max, but extra cable chest work reached RPE 9 for two sets.
- Right shoulder pain reached 8/10, likely from accumulated chest/shoulder volume:
  - Incline 3 sets at RPE 8
  - Chest Press reaching RPE 8
  - Pec Fly 3 sets, final set RPE 8
  - Lateral Raise 5kg
  - Extra cable chest sets at RPE 9
- Future rule:
  - If shoulder support appears in Chest Press or Pec Fly, no extra cable chest work.
  - If any cable/chest set reaches RPE 9, stop the exercise immediately.
  - No shoulder press after any shoulder involvement warning.
