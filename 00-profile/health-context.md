## Gym Safety Pattern
- User is in beginner comeback phase and benefits from gym, but training density can trigger lightheadedness if rest, food, hydration, or stress are not aligned.
- Important coaching rule:
  - Rest timing is a safety variable, not just a performance variable.
  - Pull days should enforce 90-120s rest between hard pull sets.
  - If sleep/rest/food are not ideal, keep first compound/pull exercises at RPE 6-7.
  - Avoid stacking too many pulldown variations at high density.
- Headphones:
  - Bluetooth headphones during gym may increase inward attention and make dizziness/anxiety easier to notice.
  - Prefer no headphones, one-ear use, or ambient mode when anxiety/body scanning is high.

## AI Coaching Implications
- Do:
  - Validate safety-first stopping as a good decision.
  - Use calm, factual language.
  - Ask for concrete symptom facts: intensity 0-10, duration, red flags, food, water, sleep, caffeine, rest intervals.
  - Help separate normal exercise sensations from warning signs.
  - Keep plans conservative when stress or dizziness appears.
- Avoid:
  - Catastrophic language.
  - Pushing through dizziness.
  - Over-medicalizing every sensation.
  - Giving medication advice beyond "track facts and discuss with doctor."
