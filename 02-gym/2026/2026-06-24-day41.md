## Movement Quality & Insights
- Key cues applied successfully:
  - Neutral wrist during pulling.
  - Hands as hooks; pull through the elbows.
  - For vertical pull: lower shoulders first, then pull elbows down toward ribs/hips.
  - For one-arm row: pull elbow toward/behind the back pocket, not just toward the torso.
- What improved today:
  - `Assisted Pull-up`: upper lat activation improved immediately once wrist position was corrected.
  - `Chest-supported Row`: good muscle burn became clear; right-side MMC was stronger than left.
  - `One-arm Cable Row`: showed strong mid-back + lat stimulus when form was clean.
  - `Rear Delt Fly`: rear delt stimulus was very clear at 10kg.
- Form lesson / realization:
  - 14kg on one-arm row was currently too heavy for full-ROM MMC work; 9kg remained useful when fatigue was high and form was corrected.
  - If shoulders take over during straight-arm pulldown, stop or reduce instead of forcing reps.
  - Left back MMC is weaker/slower than right and needs focused unilateral practice.
