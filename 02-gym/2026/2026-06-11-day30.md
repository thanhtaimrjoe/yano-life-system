## Key MMC / Form Wins
- Adapted well when Assisted Pull-up Machine was under maintenance.
- Learned that straight-bar pulldown at 23kg was too high for light lat MMC because biceps started taking over.
- Chest Supported Row at 10kg was the best-fit load: clean, controlled, no compensation.
- Neutral / V-handle Pulldown 18kg x15 x3 RPE 6 produced the clearest inner-back / lat MMC of the day.
- Right shoulder remained pain-free throughout and after the session.

## Issues / Lessons
- Straight-bar pulldown:
  - 23kg was acceptable for effort but not ideal for today’s recovery-MMC goal.
  - Next time for light day, keep straight-bar pulldown around 18kg or use V-handle first.
- Preacher Curl:
  - Set 2 already exceeded target with RPE 9 and neck/trap support.
  - Set 3 RPE 10 should be avoided on recovery/light upper days.
  - Future rule: if curl reaches RPE 9 or neck/trap supports, stop the exercise immediately.
