## Main Session

### Lat Pulldown
| Sets x Reps | Weight | RPE | Notes |
|-------------|--------|-----|-------|
| 1 x 15 | Light | 6 | Setup and form calibration |
| 1 x 13 | 20kg | 10 | Fingers and biceps dominated; load too heavy |
| 2 x 15 | 15kg | 7-8 | Improved grip; better back sensation |

#### Important Discoveries
- Pulling too slowly concentrically → increased bicep dominance.
- Slow eccentric (3s lowering) → much better lat sensation.
- Full palm grip vs finger-only grip → significantly reduced finger pain.
- "Hands are hooks" cue → improved back engagement.

#### MMC Notes
- Mild lat sensation appeared after: adjusting grip + reducing weight + pulling toward upper stomach/lower ribs + focusing on elbow drive.

---

### Seated Cable Row (Exploration)
| Sets x Reps | Weight | RPE | Notes |
|-------------|--------|-----|-------|
| 2 x 15 | 10kg | 7 | Poor lat feel; arm muscles dominated |
| Exploratory adjustments | Multiple handles | — | Tested different attachments and pulling paths |

#### Major Insight
- Attachment orientation dramatically changed recruitment pattern.
- Incorrect V-handle orientation → increased brachialis/brachioradialis dominance.
- Correcting handle orientation slightly improved lat feel.

#### Outcome
- Exercise removed from session after multiple failed setup experiments.
- Decision to move on = good autoregulation and learning decision.
